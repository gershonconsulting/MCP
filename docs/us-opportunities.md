# "Gershon" connector — US opportunities (2026-11-04). Name decided by Olivier: just "Gershon".

Goal: a foreign company's CEO plugs "Gershon US Opportunities" into their own Claude/ChatGPT and asks
"who in the US could buy from us?". The MCP answers from Gershon data and offers "Gershon can reach them for you".

Pilot buttons (public or Gershon-owned data only):
- us_events_for_industry   — graded US trade shows + exhibitors (event lead intelligence)
- new_us_companies         — newly registered/expanding US companies in the sector (E2 feed)
- us_public_opportunities  — federal tenders and grants (SAM.gov / grants.gov public data)
- book_gershon_call        — returns the booking link + a pre-filled brief of what they searched

Money: 1) lead magnet -> booked calls -> consulting/outbound retainers (main revenue);
2) optional paid tier for full contacts + weekly alerts; 3) perk for existing clients (retention).
Do NOT resell LinkedIn-scraped personal data (Radar) to third parties — ToS/privacy risk.
